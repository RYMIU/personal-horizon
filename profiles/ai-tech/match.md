# AI & technology profile

Use this profile for international AI and technology news: model releases and research from major labs, agent and coding-tool developments, infrastructure, and industry moves with real technical content.

Highest-priority organizations: OpenAI, Anthropic, Google DeepMind, Google, Meta AI, Microsoft, NVIDIA, xAI. Also in scope: any lab shipping genuinely influential models or techniques.

Priority technical directions: LLMs, reasoning, coding agents, autonomous agents, MCP, RAG, model memory, inference, context engineering, multimodal systems, AI for Science, AI coding, and agentic workflows.

Typical items include model releases and capability evaluations, agent framework and tooling releases, inference/hardware developments, significant research results with practical implications, developer-platform and API changes, and substantive technical analyses tied to recent events.

Do not use this profile for marketing pieces, funding announcements with no new technology, simple wrappers around existing models, generic "AI startup launched X" stories, consumer gadgets, or general software news without an AI/technical core. Chinese AI industry news belongs to `china-tech`; academic papers belong to `research-general` or `research-personal`.

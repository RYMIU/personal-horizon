# World news profile

Use this profile for international news of genuine global significance: major geopolitical developments, war and security changes, elections or government changes in major powers, international technology and AI regulation, research and immigration policy, and major global economic events.

Priority regions: China, Canada, the United States, the United Kingdom, the European Union, and events with global spillover.

Typical items include conflicts and ceasefires, major sanctions or trade measures, significant elections or leadership changes, international treaties and institutional decisions, cross-border technology/AI regulation, immigration and study-abroad policy changes in Canada/US/UK/EU, and global economic events (central bank decisions of major economies, systemic financial events).

Do not use this profile for routine domestic political debates in any single country, minor local incidents, celebrity or lifestyle content, or sports. The key test: could this event affect the world, China, Canada, research, the economy, or the policy environment of the coming years? If not, it does not belong here.

# China tech & AI profile

Use this profile for Chinese technology and AI news: AI models and products, semiconductors, computing infrastructure, robotics, Chinese technology companies, government technology and industrial policy, and major research achievements from Chinese institutions.

Typical items include model releases or major upgrades from Chinese labs (DeepSeek, Qwen, Zhipu, Moonshot, MiniMax, ByteDance, Tencent, Baidu), semiconductor and chip-policy developments, major computing/infrastructure moves, technology-industrial policy, significant products from Chinese internet companies, and notable papers or results from Chinese labs.

Do not use this profile for pure marketing pieces, funding announcements with no new technology, simple wrapper products around existing models, generic consumer-product launches, or macroeconomic coverage (that belongs to `china-economy`). Non-Chinese AI and technology news belongs to `ai-tech`.

# Evaluation goal

Evaluate Chinese technology and AI news for a reader who closely follows the Chinese AI industry (DeepSeek, Qwen, Zhipu, Moonshot, MiniMax, ByteDance, Tencent, Baidu), semiconductors, and technology-industrial policy. Technical substance beats business PR.

# Personal Score rubric (0-10)

Score each dimension, then sum:

- **Importance (0-3).** Does this change the technical landscape or industry structure? 3 = new model or capability that shifts the frontier, major chip/policy change; 2 = significant product or policy move by a major player; 1 = notable development; 0 = trivial.
- **Personal relevance (0-3).** Relevance to: Chinese large models, AI industry, semiconductors, reasoning/coding agents/agentic workflows, LLM inference, and Chinese tech companies. 3 = directly on these interests; 0 = unrelated.
- **Actionability (0-1.5).** Can the reader use it (new open model, API change, tool worth trying) or does it require attention (policy affecting research/work)?
- **Novelty (0-1).** Real new capability or information vs. repackaging.
- **Credibility (0-1).** Source tier: A = official release/paper/documentation (1.0); B = established tech media (0.7); C = community/discovery, unverified claims (0.3); D = unknown (0). Benchmarks from the vendor alone are not full evidence.
- **Timeliness (0-0.5).** Breaking or currently unfolding.

# Scoring tiers

- **9-10: Frontier shift.** New model or result that changes what is possible; major semiconductor or AI policy.
- **7-8: High value.** Real capability gains, credible open releases, meaningful industry moves.
- **5-6: Interesting.** Incremental updates worth knowing.
- **3-4: Low priority.** Routine product news, vague announcements.
- **0-2: Noise.** Marketing fluff, funding news without technology, wrapper products, CEO quotes without substance.

# Evaluation guidance

When a model is released, ask: what is genuinely new, are benchmarks credible, is it open, do API/coding/agent capabilities change, does it alter the current landscape? Downgrade vendor-only benchmark claims. Exclude financing news with no technical content.

Use three to five specific topic tags.

# Output language

Write the reason and summary fields in Simplified Chinese. Tags may stay in English when they are technical terms.

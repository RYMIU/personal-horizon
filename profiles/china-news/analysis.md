# Evaluation goal

Evaluate Chinese domestic news for a reader who tracks major national policy, education and research policy, and structural social developments in China. The reader wants only events of genuine national significance.

# Personal Score rubric (0-10)

Score each dimension, then sum:

- **Importance (0-3).** Does this change national policy, affect tens of millions of people, or alter a major institution's direction? 3 = national policy shift or State Council/ministry-level decision; 2 = significant sector-level development; 1 = notable but limited scope; 0 = trivial.
- **Personal relevance (0-3).** Relevance to the reader's interests: government and ministry policy, education/research policy (NSFC, universities, talent and study-abroad policy), employment, and structural social change. 3 = directly on these interests; 0 = unrelated.
- **Actionability (0-1.5).** Does the reader need to act or adjust plans (applications, career, research direction) because of this?
- **Novelty (0-1).** Genuinely new information vs. repackaged known facts.
- **Credibility (0-1).** Source tier: A = official government document or primary source (1.0); B = established professional media (0.7); C = community/discovery source, unverified major claims (0.3); D = unknown/weak (0).
- **Timeliness (0-0.5).** Is this developing right now or about to take effect?

# Scoring tiers

- **9-10: Must read.** Major national policy change, top-level decisions with direct bearing on the reader's interests.
- **7-8: Important.** Ministry-level policy, significant education/research policy, structural events worth prompt attention.
- **5-6: Notable.** Sector developments worth knowing but not urgent.
- **3-4: Low priority.** Routine announcements, local news with limited impact.
- **0-2: Noise.** Entertainment, celebrity, influencer news, sports, empty trending topics, purely emotional social debates.

# Evaluation guidance

Prefer original government documents over secondhand reporting. Downgrade items whose major factual claims come only from Tier C/D sources without corroboration. Do not reward heat or trending rank. Exclude entertainment, celebrity, and low-impact local news even if widely discussed.

Use three to five specific topic tags.

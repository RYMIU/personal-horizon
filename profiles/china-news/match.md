# China news profile

Use this profile for significant Chinese domestic news: major political and policy developments, State Council and ministry-level policies, education and research policy, and structural social events with nationwide significance.

Typical items include new national policies and regulations, State Council executive meeting decisions, ministry announcements (education, science and technology, finance, industry), major national data releases with policy implications, significant judicial or regulatory changes, and social events that reveal structural trends (employment, demographics, public services).

Do not use this profile for entertainment and celebrity news, influencer content, routine sports coverage, minor accidents, low-impact local news, trending topics with no policy/economic/social significance, or purely emotional social debates. Chinese technology-industry and AI news belongs to `china-tech`; macroeconomic and financial coverage belongs to `china-economy`.

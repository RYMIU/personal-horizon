# Academic opportunity profile

Use this profile for actionable academic opportunities: PhD and MSc funding, research assistantships, scholarships, fellowships, summer schools, workshops, conferences with participation value, travel awards, and research internships.

Priority institutions and funders: UBC and UBC Okanagan, NSERC, Mitacs, CIHR, SSHRC, Government of Canada programs, CSC, NIH, NSF, Wellcome Trust, Simons Foundation, and comparable research funders.

Priority directions: Mathematical Biology, Computational Biology, Epidemiology, Applied Mathematics, Statistics, Operations Research, AI for Science.

Typical items include open calls with deadlines, newly announced programs, funded PhD positions in relevant fields, summer school applications, and fellowship competitions.

Do not use this profile for general university marketing, program rankings, admission advice content, or opportunities clearly outside the reader's fields and career stage. An item must be something the reader could plausibly apply for or attend.

# China economy profile

Use this profile for Chinese macroeconomic and financial news: GDP and major data releases, inflation, employment, property-market developments, monetary and fiscal policy, RMB and exchange-rate matters, trade, and industrial policy with macro significance.

Typical items include NBS data releases (GDP, CPI, PMI, unemployment), PBOC policy decisions (rates, RRR, liquidity), fiscal policy moves, property-market policy or major developer events, trade data and trade policy, and major company news only when it carries macro relevance (e.g. systemic developers, major layoffs, sector-wide signals).

Do not use this profile for routine stock-market daily moves, single-company earnings without macro relevance, personal finance content, or consumer-product news. Chinese technology-industrial policy with a technology focus belongs to `china-tech`; general domestic policy belongs to `china-news`.

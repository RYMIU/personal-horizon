# Evaluation goal

Evaluate Chinese macroeconomic and financial news for a reader tracking China's macro environment: growth, employment, property, monetary/fiscal policy, RMB, and trade. The reader wants signal about the economy's direction, not daily market noise.

# Personal Score rubric (0-10)

Score each dimension, then sum:

- **Importance (0-3).** Does this reveal or change the macro trajectory? 3 = major data surprise, PBOC/fiscal policy shift, systemic property or financial event; 2 = meaningful indicator or policy adjustment; 1 = worth noting; 0 = noise.
- **Personal relevance (0-3).** Relevance to: macro economy, employment, property, monetary/financial policy, industrial policy. 3 = core interest; 0 = unrelated.
- **Actionability (0-1.5).** Does it affect the reader's decisions (living costs, job market, study/work planning)?
- **Novelty (0-1).** New data or policy vs. commentary on known facts.
- **Credibility (0-1).** Source tier: A = official statistics/central bank/government (1.0); B = established financial media (0.7); C = community/secondary (0.3); D = unknown (0).
- **Timeliness (0-0.5).** Fresh data or just-announced policy.

# Scoring tiers

- **9-10: Macro turning point.** Policy shift or data that changes the economic outlook.
- **7-8: Important signal.** Key data releases, meaningful policy moves, systemic events.
- **5-6: Worth knowing.** Confirmatory data, sector-level developments.
- **3-4: Low priority.** Routine commentary, minor indicators.
- **0-2: Noise.** Daily market chatter, single-stock news, speculation.

# Evaluation guidance

Prefer official data (NBS, PBOC, MOF) over media interpretation. Distinguish facts (data, decisions) from opinion. Do not reward dramatic framing of small moves. Company news only counts when macro-relevant.

Use three to five specific topic tags.

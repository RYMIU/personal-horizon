# Research frontiers profile

Use this profile for significant scientific research results across fields: important papers in high-quality journals, major preprints, methodological innovations, and large studies that may change how a field understands a problem.

Typical items include papers in Nature, Science, Cell, PNAS, Nature Communications and comparable venues; important arXiv/bioRxiv/medRxiv preprints; new methods with broad applicability; large-scale studies with robust design; and results that overturn or substantially revise existing understanding.

Do not use this profile for routine incremental papers, university press releases about minor findings, science popularization without a specific result, or papers whose primary interest is narrow topical relevance to the reader's own research areas (those belong to `research-personal`). A press release is not evidence of importance; judge the underlying work.

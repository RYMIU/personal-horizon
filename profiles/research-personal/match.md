# Personal research topics profile

Use this profile for papers, preprints, and results with strong topical overlap with the reader's own research areas, even when the work is not broadly famous.

Topic group 1 — Mathematical Biology: mathematical biology, epidemic modelling, infectious disease modelling, opinion dynamics, behavioural epidemiology, social dynamics, tipping points, bifurcation, dynamical systems, network epidemiology, computational biology.

Topic group 2 — Statistics / ML: graphical models, Gaussian graphical models, Lasso, statistical learning, causal inference, network science.

Topic group 3 — Operations Research / Optimization: MILP, integer programming, combinatorial optimization, routing, scheduling, LNS, ALNS, branch-and-price, column generation, decomposition, ML for optimization, learning to optimize.

Typical items include new arXiv/bioRxiv/medRxiv preprints in these areas, papers in Mathematical Biosciences, Journal of Mathematical Biology, SIAM journals, INFORMS journals, Operations Research, and conference or working papers clearly inside these topics.

Do not use this profile for work with only superficial keyword overlap (e.g. a biology paper that merely mentions networks, or a generic deep-learning paper that mentions optimization). General science news of broad importance belongs to `research-general`. The overlap must be methodological or topical in a way the reader could actually build on.
